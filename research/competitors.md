## 3. 用户原话：他们爱什么、恨什么、缺什么

### 爱（为什么留下来）
- "knowing my Dom can see what I've done each day gives me a sense of accountability" (4★ 2026-05)
- "When I get reward points and see 'I'm proud of you baby' my entire day changes!" (5★ 2024-10)
- "I get a reminder at night to make sure I've done my tasks. My Dom knows when I complete them as he gets an alert. It's exactly what we needed." (5★ 2024-02)
- "It helps us flip the switch back" ——忙碌 vanilla 生活后切回动态 (4★ 2025-01)
- "Task management can be a huge chore especially for the neurodivergent brain… I love that my partner and I can have access and limits can be established of who can edit what. It gives it yet another D/s feel." (4★ 2025-04)
- "We used to just text/note share my tasks, but I have ADHD & would often forget… till the end of the day or not at all." (4★ 2024-07)
- "My wife and I don't even use it for kink (yet)… I haven't been able to find any other app that allows for consequences for failing." (5★ 2024-04)
- "we took some of the templates and tailored them to what works for us" (5★ 2025-01)

### 恨（为什么走）
- 付费：见上。"Having habits be the ONLY feature that requires two subscriptions is so messed up"
- 数据不可信："Yesterday I was at 26 but when I clicked into it today I was at 9 and I know it's not my Dom doing it" (1★ 2025-12)
- 每天打开都被弹付费："IT ASK YOU EVERY TIME YOU OPEN THE APP" (2★ 2025-09)
- 时区 / 一天何时开始："I go to bed late and having to update the history for several of my tasks is annoying" (4★ 2026-05)
- **审批时限压在 Dom 身上**："If the dominant doesn't approve the task by the time they set for the submissive, it counts the task as incomplete. That's not at all how this should work… should be able to trust the submissive enough to review at the dominant's convenience." (2★ 2024-02)；另一条 "I completed mine before it was due but my dom didn't approve it until after the due time so I got points docked" (5★ 2024-12)
- 循环任务必须绑定具体时间/日期 (1★ 2026-05)

### 缺（明确提出的功能诉求）
- **送达/待审回执**："when you submitted a photo it would let you know it was received and is waiting approval from your Dom. Sometimes I submit photos and cannot remember later if I already did. From my end it looks empty." (4★ 2025-04)
- 文字提交（不只照片）
- 迟交 / 申请延期："if subs could request late tasks or send in late tasks for a penalty" (5★ 2024-12)
- 无截止日期的一次性 bonus 任务："requires the submissive to be motivated to do it on their own without the pressure of a deadline" (4★ 2024-05)
- Dom 自由裁量给分："the Dom could award reward points at his discretion unrelated to habits" (4★ 2025-01)
- 奖励兑换需 Dom 批准 (4★ 2025-03)
- widget（ADHD："out of sight is out of mind"）
- 坏习惯（要戒的）不应被提醒去"完成" (5★ 2025-11)
- 不知道惩罚怎么被"触发" (4★ 2024-03)
- Dom 自己的待办 / 提醒；co-dom；每日多次 check-in（Kneel）；备注带 "acknowledged / actioned" 按钮（Kneel）；设备级私密锁（Kneel）

## 5. Reddit 对工具的态度（r/BDSMAdvice 3 帖，2026-01/05）

- 支持 App：Obedience "all but mandatory when I had multiple partners… two jobs"；"my [Dom] can send me reminders if I haven't completed things by a certain time… rewards and punishments… all point based"；"You get notified when your sub does or doesn't do task"
- 反对 App（资深 TPE Dom）："Ditch the apps completely… Stand over her while she writes a check list that you dictate the night before… at the end of the following day, have her kneel and go over what got complete… Tech… took the soul out of daily"
- 中间派："A shared Google doc or spreadsheet has served me well"；"A simple notes app… Fill out each night and send to dom… discreet"
- 积分系统的病："points are way too easy to earn, so rewards don't really feel rewarding… punishments don't always happen immediately, so they end up piling up"；解法建议："base-level healthy habits shouldn't earn points, only failure punished"
- "the App we're using has limited functionality so we can't do too many tasks at once"（免费 5 条上限）

## 6. 结论：桌面筹码 / 差异点 / 没人做

**桌面筹码（不做就不被考虑）**：两人配对；每日/每周循环任务 + 单次任务；完成打勾；对方完成时的通知；某种奖励机制（积分或非积分）；提醒；照片/文字提交；模板库帮新手起步；一次付费或单方付费。

**现有产品的共同伤口**：
1. 双人订阅 → 付费墙 → 弹窗骚扰（Obedience、Kneel、EverCollar、BeMoreKinky 全部中招）
2. 数据不可信（积分乱跳、完成状态跨天错乱、时区）
3. 把 Dom 变成打卡机器：审批有截止、积分自动扣、Dom 没有自己的一侧

**用户要了但没人做**：
- 提交后的"已送达 / 待看"回执
- 迟交、求饶、延期——作为一等操作而不是 bug
- Dom 可以在任何时间审阅，不被系统计时
- 记录上的对话（Kizuna 的"在日历里留言求饶/处罚"、Kneel 用户要的"acknowledged / actioned"）
- Dom 自己的待办与提醒
- 不带截止的自愿加分任务

**反面教材**：Our Dynamic 用"不计分、赞美优先、认可来自伴侣而非 App"做卖点，几乎没有用户。哲学正确不等于有人要；用户口中最高频的正向词是 **points / rewards / reminder / alert / accountability**。
